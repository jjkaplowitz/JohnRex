About

A Leader
Built on Service

John-Rex Spivey is a native of Rogers, Arkansas, and a 2013 graduate of the United States Naval Academy, where he earned a B.S. in History, lettered on the Championship Sprint Football team, and was elected President of the Class of 2013 — a position he continues to hold today.

After earning his Wings of Gold as a Navy Pilot, he deployed twice in support of Operation Odyssey Lightning and participated in multiple humanitarian aid and disaster-relief missions with HSC-22.

“Some men see things as they are and ask why. Others dream things that never were and ask why not.”

— George Bernard Shaw

Today, John-Rex leads EEI Strategic Advisors, translating decades of military, congressional, and international experience into actionable strategy — with a particular focus on his home state of Arkansas.

The Natural State

Rooted in
Arkansas

A native of Rogers, John-Rex’s vision for EEI is inseparable from his commitment to his home state. His mission: make Arkansas the model for economic development in rural America — building a repeatable framework to address the critical challenges of our time.

Arkansas combines a unique ecosystem of logistics, agriculture, defense, and natural resources. John-Rex believes this foundation — paired with the state’s values, pride, and community — positions Arkansas for transformative, generational growth.
